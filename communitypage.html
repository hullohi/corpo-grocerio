    Who could it be for?

    What could it be about?

    Critiquing big corporations’ hold in the food market; especially what is easily accessible to us via grocery stores.

    It is satirical, interactive (game and charts), and I feel like people would look twice at some of the website’s elements.

    Where would it take place?

    Online and in a game; inspired by irl

    How could it be accessed?

    How could it be used?

    It can be used as a reminder of corporations’ control on food. How are our basic needs commodified and do they do it respectfully?

    How could it be used?
